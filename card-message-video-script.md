# Organic Video #1 — "Nobody Sends Cards Anymore"

**Format:** Short-form vertical (Reels / TikTok / Shorts), ~50–60 seconds
**Goal:** Introduce the problem the company solves. Organic — NO call to action.
**Opening visual:** Talent is mid-writing on a card when the video starts. Don't look up at the camera right away — let the pen do the first second of talking.

---

## THE SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00–0:06)
*Visual: Close-ish shot. You're writing on a card. Camera's already rolling — this should feel like we caught you in the middle of it. Look up on the second line.*

> Be honest — when's the last time you got one of these?
>
> Not a text. Not a DM. An actual card. Real handwriting. In your actual mailbox.

### THE PROBLEM (0:06–0:22)
*Visual: Put the pen down, talk to camera. Keep the card in frame on the table.*

> Exactly. And it's not because people stopped caring — it's because nobody has time.
>
> Think about what sending a card actually takes. Drive to the store. Stare at the card aisle for ten minutes. Figure out what to write. Find their address. Find a stamp — who owns stamps? — and then actually get it to a mailbox.
>
> That's like an hour of your life. So we all do the easy thing instead: "happy birthday!! 🎉" ...send.

### WHY IT MATTERS (0:22–0:38)
*Visual: Pick the card back up. Slow down your delivery here — this is the emotional beat.*

> But here's the thing. A text gets buried in about thirty seconds.
>
> A card? A card sits on someone's counter for three weeks. It goes on the fridge. Some people keep them in a box for years.
>
> It's the difference between "I remembered you" and "I made time for you." And people can feel that difference the second they open the envelope.

### THE REVEAL (0:38–0:52)
*Visual: Back to writing as you say this — casual, like it's an afterthought, not a pitch.*

> That's actually what I've been working on. People send me the message they wish they had time to send — and I write it out by hand, on a real card, and mail it for them.
>
> They get the credit. Their person gets something they'll actually keep. Everybody wins.

*(Alt line if someone other than the founder is on camera: "That's actually what my buddy started doing. You send him the message — he handwrites it on a real card and mails it for you.")*

### CLOSE — no CTA (0:52–0:58)
*Visual: Finish the card, slide it into the envelope, seal it. Small smile. Cut before it feels like a pitch.*

> Anyway — this one's headed to somebody's grandma in Ohio.
>
> She's gonna lose it.

## ALTERNATE HOOKS (test these across future cuts)

1. **The stat-style hook:** "You send about 100 texts a day. When's the last time any of them ended up on someone's fridge?"
2. **The confession hook:** "I used to be a 'happy birthday 🎉' text guy. Then I watched my mom cry over a $4 card."
3. **The contrarian hook:** "Sending a card in 2026 is a superpower, and I can prove it."

## B-ROLL / SHOT LIST

- Macro shot of pen on cardstock (great for the hook and the close)
- Sealing the envelope + stamp going on
- A stack of finished envelopes
- Phone screen showing a generic "happy birthday!!" text (the "easy thing" beat)
- A card standing up on a kitchen counter or fridge (the "it sits there for weeks" beat)

## DELIVERY NOTES

- Talk like you're telling one friend, not an audience. Contractions, incomplete sentences — all good.
- The "who owns stamps?" line is the laugh beat. Don't rush past it.
- Slow down noticeably at "I made time for you." That line is the whole video.
- The reveal should sound like you almost forgot to mention it. The second it sounds like an ad, organic is dead.
- End cold after "She's gonna lose it." No logo card, no "follow for more." The comments will ask what the site is — that's the point.

## CAPTION IDEA

> a text takes 10 seconds. a card gets kept for 10 years.
